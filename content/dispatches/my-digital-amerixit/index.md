I’m always suspicious when someone tells me they’re decent and honest. Silence is a virtue and virtue is silent. Which is to say: i’ve never been crazy about Tim Cook. If he really believed in the teachings of Martin Luther King Jr, he wouldn’t just sell colourful Apple Watch bands, but make the effort to get at least one African-American person on [his leadership team](https://www.apple.com/leadership/). If he had to endure the actual consequences of homophobia, he wouldn’t just sell colourful Apple Watch bands, but [speak out](https://joe-steel.com/2025-01-25-Tim-Cook-Is-Failing-Us.html) against the efforts to systematically discriminate against members of the LGBTQ communities.

But those watch bands sure are colourful, and representation matters, so i thought his grandstanding was a necessary evil. Turns out, he’s no evil, just another immensely privileged and intensely bland number crusher. I can’t deny he’s good at his soul-crushing job: he bought his seat at Trump’s table for [a mere million dollars](https://www.axios.com/2025/01/03/tim-cook-apple-donate-1-million-trump-inauguration) and only had [to feature an openly fascist ‘social’ network](https://social.lol/@scottwillsey/114310995564258839) on the App Store to get a tax reprieve. What things you can accomplish when your only moral compass is the sacrosanct ‘shareholder value maximization’! A friendship of convenience is still a friendship, which means Tim Cook has to be described as a friend of Trump’s. A trade war is still a war, which means Donald Trump is my (entire continent’s) enemy. A friend of an enemy can’t be my friend.

Even though i own a [Fairphone 5](https://shop.fairphone.com/fairphone-5), i can’t ditch my iPhone, not while i’m still employed as a tech journalist anyway. However, i can do everything in my power to minimize Apple’s hold on it and maximize my use of European alternatives. The bulk of the work is done by [Proton](https://pr.tn/ref/W6VDWS83Q3P0):

- i was already using [Proton Mail](https://pr.tn/ref/W6VDWS83Q3P0) for my personal e-mail;
- i’ll switch to [Proton Calendar](https://proton.me/calendar) at the end of the school year;
- i’ve imported all of my files into [Proton Drive](https://proton.me/drive);
- it took me all of ten minutes to switch to [Proton Pass](https://proton.me/pass);
- [Proton VPN](https://protonvpn.com/) is a nice side benefit.

Proton operates out of Switzerland, just like [Infomaniak](https://www.infomaniak.com/goto/fr/home?utm_term=5fd3bff1d1501) (where i host all of my websites and get all of my domain names) and Silvio Rizzi (the developer of the wonderful [Reeder](https://reeder.app/) feed reader and the cute [Mela](https://mela.recipes/) recipe manager). Lest i be accused of betraying my own country, let it be known that i listen to music on [Qobuz](https://www.qobuz.com/fr-fr/discover) with [Audirvana](https://audirvana.com/), watch movies on [Canal+](https://www.canalplus.com/), block ads with [NextDNS](https://nextdns.io/?from=vmjyb7pc) and ride my (Dutch) bike with [Geovelo](https://geovelo.app/fr/). I write in a German app, [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/), and draw in a Dutch one, [Sketch](https://www.sketch.com/). My [Oura](https://ouraring.com/) ring comes from Finland, which is also where i get [my weather updates](https://www.foreca.com/). For the last few weeks, i’ve been using a Norwegian browser, [Vivaldi](https://vivaldi.com/).

It might well be impossible to use only European apps and services, but it’s not implausible to imagine living a digital life without American ones. My trusty [Antidote](https://www.antidote.info/en/) and the awesome [Transit](https://transitapp.com/) are from Québec, [GoodLinks](https://goodlinks.app/) is from Vietnam, [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/) is from New Zealand and [The Storygraph](https://app.thestorygraph.com/) is from the United Kingdom, which may or may not count as a European country. I’m happy to see that a lot of the open-source software i’ve come to rely on has European roots, such as [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/) (Netherlands) and [Organic Maps](https://organicmaps.app/) (Estonia), not to mention Linux of course (Finland).

Which leaves me with a few intractable issues:

- [Qwant](https://www.qwant.com/?l=en) and [Ecosia](https://www.ecosia.org/) still aren’t good enough search engines that i can consider moving away from Kagi;
- i’ve yet to find a photo app that combines syncing and editing. I might have to go back to two separate apps, in which case i’ll probably default to Proton Drive for syncing and [DxO PhotoLab](https://www.dxo.com/fr/dxo-photolab/) for editing;
- i really don’t want to switch from Duolingo to [Babbel](https://uk.babbel.com/);
- there don’t seem to be any European podcast players left.

It could be worse — I could be living in the United States.
