Nothing they predicted for 2025, let alone 2026, has come to pass. They’ve now pushed the “reliable agent” a full year, even it’s not been proven that what they were calling “stumbling agents” are even a thing. They still don’t understand that finding exploits in network-accessible systems is exponentionally easier than ordering a pizza at my local place. That’s software brain at its paroxysm.

But OK, let’s pretend we should pay any attention to what they’re laying out. Their plan A (and worse plan S) have no chance of working because there’s never been a way to turn back EVER. Effet de cliquet is real. So it’s out, which means that in their view, humanity will be extinct in TK years.

Which is to say: this is plain batshit crazy. Superintelligence is still the idea that eats smart people. This website reads as bad SF mired in a vague messianic TK and an atrociously bad grip on economics. And physics – we have not enough resources and energy in our entire Solar system to sustain what they’re talking about.

They’re so intent on killing us all before the end of the century that they forget that people *now*. And people are asking a simple question: why. Why would you do that?

That’s a powerful question, and it generally ends in revolutions. I truly do think that we’re heading for a violent recknoning in the world of AI, because people really want to know why.

Why losing jobs for hallucinations.
Why everything is more expensive even though hasn’t proved it has any material effect on the world.
Why we’re burning oil at an unprecented rate TK.
Why we’re still pretending that language is (super-)intelligence when countless politicians have proven that it’s not. 

In a sentence: why are still pretending that AI is the future when it’s clearly destroying the present? Well, it’s clear why. (Paraphrase French philosopher - see Kobo books and Le Monde article.) That’s why. We’re not using those powerful systems to TK, TK, TK, TK, and generally make computing easier. We’re using to destroy the world more effectively, all for a small caste to make more money. Call me an optimist, but i’m not sure that people are on board with that. (Links to previous revolutions.)
